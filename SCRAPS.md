# Inspector's scraps // hard edition

*These notes are commitments, not a blueprint. They were filed in the order found, not in the order used.*

**SCRAP E — Jury:** A witness is not yet a verdict. First the colors speak together. Then the glass may alter what they said. Only afterward do the jurors decide.

**SCRAP B — Ledger:** The Archive preserves a fixed-width seal of the whole wall. A square that looks empty is still a square in the ledger.

**SCRAP F — Oath:** A correct key can still fail at the door. The oath is short, exact, and intolerant of missing rails or trailing breath.

**SCRAP C — Receipts:** The bright cells are data, not decoration. Six receipts, four bytes each. Read each tile consistently, and keep the illustrated order.

**SCRAP A — Ticket:** The city keeps its original addresses. The railway does not rename them. A path is chosen from admissible stations without revisiting the same one.

**SCRAP D — Forge:** The jars and the forge consume material, not displays. The labels matter exactly as written, and one dial measures growth by powers.

**Last note:** Every seal in this repository can reject an error, but none can reveal the letter by itself.
